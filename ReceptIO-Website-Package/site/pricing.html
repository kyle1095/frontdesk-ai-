FAQ

Common questions

What counts as a conversation?
Each new visitor chat session with the widget counts as one conversation, however many questions they ask within it.
What happens if I go over my monthly limit?
You'll get a polite prompt to upgrade. Nothing cuts off mid-conversation — every chat in progress finishes normally.
How do I cancel?
Cancel anytime from your account settings. You're only billed for the time you used, prorated.
